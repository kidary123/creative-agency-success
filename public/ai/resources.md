# Resources — Creative Agency Success

Free and public resources for creative-agency owners, outside of the paid programs.

## Facebook Group

The community behind "find your tribe" — a peer group of creative-agency owners sharing what is working, what is not, and what they are stuck on. This is where Collective Confidence starts for most founders before they ever join a program.

## Books

Long-form writing on the fundamentals of agency economics: positioning, pricing, service design, operations, and the structural decisions that determine whether an agency is a job or a company.

## Free Trainings

Workshops covering the pillars of [The Scale Method](/ai/scale-method.md) — positioning exercises, service-line audits, operational structure, and the mechanics of moving to recurring revenue.

## Podcast

Conversations with agency owners about scaling, profit, and freedom. Real numbers and real constraints from founders at every stage between 5 figures and $10M+.
