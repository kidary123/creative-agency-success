**$3,000,000 revenue added in just 90 days** — the result of installing a predictable client-acquisition system rather than depending on referrals and inbound chance.

**$1,500,000 profit per partner per year** — profit, not revenue. This is the figure that reflects the Specialization and Recurring Revenue pillars: margin retained rather than gross billings.

**897% revenue increase in 9 months** — the compounding effect of Positioning and Authority working together once an agency stops competing on price.

**83% efficiency gains** — the Seamless Operation pillar measured directly: the same output delivered with dramatically less founder involvement and less internal friction.
